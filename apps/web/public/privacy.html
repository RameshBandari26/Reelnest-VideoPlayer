Reelnest privacy policy

Last updated: REPLACE-WITH-DATE

Reelnest plays video and music files that are already on your device. It does not collect, upload or sell your files or personal data. There are no accounts and no ads.

What stays on your device

  • Your files are played locally. They are never sent to our servers.
  • The app remembers your library list, resume positions and settings on your own device (in the app's storage, or in your browser's local storage for the web version).

Permissions

The Android app asks for access to audio and video on your device so it can list and play them. This access is used only for playback inside the app.

Links you paste

If you paste a link to a video or song, your device connects directly to that website to stream it. That website's own policies apply.
